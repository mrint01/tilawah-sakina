Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Tilawah Sakina Poster is a private tool that publishes Quran recitation and tasbih videos to the Instagram account @tilawah.sakina, which is run by the same owner as the tool.

What data the tool uses

The tool only accesses the @tilawah.sakina account itself: it publishes videos with their captions and reads basic numbers for those posts (views, likes, comments). It does not collect, store or share any data about other Instagram or Facebook users.

Where data is kept

Post IDs and links of published videos are kept in the project's private repository so the owner can see what was posted. Nothing is sold or given to third parties.

Data deletion

The tool holds no personal data about anyone. To ask for anything to be removed, send a direct message to @tilawah.sakina on Instagram and it will be deleted within 30 days.

Contact

Direct message @tilawah.sakina on Instagram.
